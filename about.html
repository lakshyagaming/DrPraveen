Meet Dr. Praveen Kumar

Dr. Praveen Kumar is a highly experienced and deeply dedicated General Physician practicing at Mukhiya G Plaza, Accher, Greater Noida. With over 18 years of clinical experience, he has built a reputation as one of the most trusted and patient-friendly doctors in the region.

He completed his MBBS degree and has since dedicated himself to providing comprehensive, compassionate primary healthcare to thousands of patients across Greater Noida, Noida Extension, and surrounding areas. His approach combines modern medical practice with a genuine understanding of each patient's unique needs and circumstances.

Dr. Kumar is known for his thorough examinations, clear communication, and evidence-based prescriptions. He believes in educating patients about their conditions and involving them in treatment decisions — making healthcare a collaborative journey rather than a one-sided transaction.
