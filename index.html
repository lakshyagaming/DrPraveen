Book Appointment
What are the clinic timings? ▼
The clinic is open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM (morning) and 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM (evening). Sunday consultations are available on prior appointment.
How do I book an appointment? ▼
You can book by calling +91 8585912258, sending a WhatsApp message, or using our online appointment form on this website. Walk-ins are also welcome subject to availability.
Does Dr. Praveen treat children? ▼
Yes, Dr. Praveen Kumar provides general medical care for all age groups including children, adults, and senior citizens. For specialized pediatric care, referrals are made as needed.
Are home visits available? ▼
Home visits are available for elderly or immobile patients in select areas around Accher and Greater Noida. Please call in advance to check availability.
What payment methods are accepted? ▼
We accept cash, UPI (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm), and major debit/credit cards. Consultation fees are very affordable to make healthcare accessible to all.
