Fever & Infection Management

Fever is one of the most common reasons patients visit a general physician. Dr. Praveen Kumar has extensive experience in accurately diagnosing the cause of fever and providing targeted treatment. From simple viral fevers to more complex bacterial infections like typhoid, he ensures rapid recovery.

Using clinical assessment and necessary blood investigations, Dr. Praveen differentiates between various fever types and prescribes the most appropriate treatment — avoiding unnecessary antibiotics while ensuring effective care.

✓ Viral & bacterial fever
✓ Typhoid fever management
✓ Dengue & malaria screening
✓ Paediatric fever care
